How AI assists with detection

When a photo is uploaded, an AI model scans it for visible signs of road damage and suggests a likely category with a confidence score. These are preliminary detections, not confirmed findings — every complaint is still reviewed by a municipal administrator before any action is taken.

AI analysis will be available once a trained detection model is connected. Until then, every complaint can still be submitted and is routed for manual review.

About Road Rakshak

Road Rakshak is a civic technology demonstration platform that lets citizens report road damage and lets municipal staff track and manage repairs. It is built as a modular Flask application and is being developed in stages; this build represents the foundation — project structure, database models, and the landing page.
